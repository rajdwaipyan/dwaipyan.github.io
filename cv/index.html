Professional summary

Technology professional and NGO executive based in Dhaka. Chief Executive Officer of Surjamukhi Sangstha (SMS) since 26 October 2026, after leading the organisation's IT and projects from January 2020, including programme activities involving the International Labour Organization and Dhaka South City Corporation. Co-founder and operational CEO of ED Shield Solutions. More than six years of web development for international clients — WordPress, WooCommerce, Shopify, web applications and CRM automation with GoHighLevel and n8n. Professional Masters in Information Technology (Jahangirnagar University) and B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering (Stamford University Bangladesh).
