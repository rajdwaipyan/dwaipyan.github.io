02 Services

Three things I do, and how they connect.

Most projects start with one of these and grow into another: a website that needs to feed a CRM, or a workflow that needs a proper dashboard. Having one person who understands all three keeps the pieces working together.

01 — Website design & development

A website that's clear, quick and yours to run.

I design and build business websites on WordPress, usually with Elementor Pro so your team can edit pages without calling a developer. That covers new builds, redesigns of sites that have become slow or hard to update, and WooCommerce stores when you sell online.

  • You're launching, rebranding or replacing a site that no longer reflects the business
  • Your current site is slow, breaks on mobile or is painful to edit
  • You need forms, bookings or a shop wired into the tools you already use

Capabilities WordPress · Elementor Pro · custom layouts · redesigns · speed & responsive fixes · WooCommerce · maintenance

Tools that replace the spreadsheet maze.

Every lead lands, and someone follows up.

I work with GoHighLevel and n8n, and with any tool that offers an API or webhooks.

  • Leads arrive by form, email and phone and some get missed
  • Someone copies data between tools by hand every week
  • You've bought GoHighLevel but haven't set it up properly

Capabilities GoHighLevel · n8n · pipelines & follow-up sequences · webhooks · REST APIs · form and CRM integration

03 How I work

From first message to launch, no guesswork.

  1. 01

    Understand

    You send a short brief. I read it, look at what you have today and come back with questions or a call slot.

  2. 02

    Scope

    A written scope: what's included, what isn't, the timeline and the price. Nothing starts until you've agreed it.

  3. 03

    Build

    Work happens in stages on a private preview link, so you see progress and can steer early instead of at the end.

  4. 04

    Test

    Forms, integrations, speed and every screen size get checked before anything goes live.

  5. 05

    Launch & hand over

    Go-live, a walkthrough of how to manage it, and support afterwards if you want it.

04 About

A developer who also thinks about the business.

I'm Dwaipyan Mandal (sometimes written Dwaipayan Mandal), a web developer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I build WordPress websites, custom web applications and the CRM and workflow automations that connect them, for clients in Europe, North America and the Pacific.

I studied Computer Science & Engineering at Stamford University Bangladesh and went on to a master's in Information Technology at the Institute of Information Technology, Jahangirnagar University. Before focusing on client development, I led IT for a Bangladeshi NGO, including programmes delivered with the ILO and Dhaka South City Corporation. That work taught me to scope carefully, report clearly and deliver to fixed deadlines.

Today I'm co-founder of ED Shield Solutions, a web design and development company in Dhaka. I also build the tools we run on ourselves, including WordPress plugins for staff attendance and expense tracking.
