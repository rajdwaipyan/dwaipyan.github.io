One site. Four layers deep.

Most projects start as a website and grow into something that sells, serves clients and follows up on its own. Scroll through how one site gets there.

  1. 01 — Websites

    A site people trust in five seconds.

  2. 02 — Online stores

    Then it sells.

    Products, bookings and checkout that work on a phone: WooCommerce when the store lives inside WordPress, Shopify when it should stand on its own.

  3. 03 — Web applications

    Then it serves people who log in.

    Client portals, dashboards and internal tools that replace the spreadsheet maze — inside WordPress as a custom plugin when that fits, or as a standalone app when it doesn't.

    Then it follows up on its own.

    Every form, order and booking lands in your CRM, gets tagged and routed, and triggers the right email, text or team alert — without anyone copying data by hand.

    GoHighLevel · n8n · webhooks · REST APIs · AI-assisted workflows
