What you ask

Write a product description for our new running shoe.

One line. No audience, no voice, no format — so every answer comes out different.

What the AI needs to know

  • Role E-commerce copywriter
  • Audience Weekend runners, 25–45
  • Voice Confident, no hype
  • Facts Weight, drop, foam, price
  • Limits No invented claims
  • Format Title · 3 bullets · 60 words

The engineered instruction

ROLE      E-commerce copywriter for a running brand.
AUDIENCE  Weekend runners aged 25–45.
FACTS     Use only: 240 g · 8 mm drop · [foam] · $129.
RULES     No medical claims. No "revolutionary".
          If a fact is missing, write [CHECK].
FORMAT    Title (≤ 8 words), 3 benefit bullets,
          one closing line. 60 words in total.

The kind of answer it produces illustration

Light Enough for Every Weekend Mile

  • 240 g, so your legs stay fresh past the 10 km mark.
  • An 8 mm drop for a smooth, natural stride.
  • [CHECK] foam name — cushioning that keeps its bounce.

Built for the runs you plan all week. $129.
