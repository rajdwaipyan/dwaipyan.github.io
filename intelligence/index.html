    04 How it works

    Strongest evidence first.

    Every item carries one of six labels, so a claim is never dressed up as a fact. The map reads the same data files as the rest of the site; when they change, it changes.

    • Live project A client website that is online — open it and look.
    • Record A degree, an accepted report or a formal appointment.
    • Described on this site A role, course or programme described on these pages.
    • Self-described A skill I list myself — ask me for examples.
    • Stated, not yet documented True, but not yet written up here with dates or detail.
    • Goal A direction or work in progress — not an achievement.

    What this is not

    It is not a chatbot and it does not use an AI model. Your words are matched against the labels, tools, industries and places in the data, and it tells you which words matched. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

    If something you need isn't here, ask me directly.
